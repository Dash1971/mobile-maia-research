# Research publication

Publish Mobile Maia research reports, recorded data, figures and reproduction tools in this GitHub research repository, separate from the application repositories. App READMEs should contain only brief decision summaries and links to published research.

Preserve measurement provenance, source pins and limitations. Never present the project's design choices as upstream recommendations without direct evidence. Keep model weights, private local paths, environments and credentials out of publications. Verify reported numbers and links before publishing.
